SEVEN-BALL · HOUSE RULES

Shared-table pool, not standard 8-ball. Seven numbered object balls. Alternate one shot each, even after a pot. Every legal pot is one point, not the ball’s number.

Scratch? No points for that whole shot. Potted object balls stay down; the cue ball respots. Clear all seven to finish. Higher score wins; equal scores draw.

Simplified rails and pockets. No called pockets or first-contact fouls.
