CASUAL 8-BALL · HOUSE RULES

Not full official/WPA rules, and not an exact Messenger rules replica. The first clean non-eight pot assigns solids (1–7) or stripes (9–15), including on the break. If both groups fall, the first potted ball decides.

Hit your group first; after clearing it, hit the eight first. A clean own-group pot keeps your turn. A miss, wrong first contact, no contact or scratch passes it. Potted balls stay down even on fouls.

Sink the eight only when your whole group was already gone before the shot. Early eight, foul eight, or scratch with the eight loses. A legal eight wins. No called pockets, rail-after-contact requirement or special break rule. 200 shots is a draw.

Automatic-respot house rule: scratches replace the cue in the first clear spot; no manual ball-in-hand. Other fouls leave the cue where it rests. Simplified v5 rails and circular pockets are unchanged.

AIM & PLAY

Tap or drag the felt to aim, adjust Fine aim and the vertical power meter, then shoot. Portrait: 0° down, 90° left, 180° up, 270° right. Arrow keys adjust sliders. Settings offers Instant or Full rolling.
