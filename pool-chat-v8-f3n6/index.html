CASUAL 8-BALL · HOUSE RULES

Clear your solids (1–7) or stripes (9–15), then call the numbered pocket for the eight. Early eight, scratch with eight, foul eight or wrong final pocket loses. Legal called eight wins.

The table remains open after the break. First clean non-eight pot after the break assigns groups; if both groups fall, first potted decides. A clean own-group pot means you must shoot again before sharing. A miss ends your turn.

Scratch, wrong first contact, no object contact, or no rail contact after contact when nothing is potted: foul. Opponent gets ball in hand anywhere clear on the felt. Tap/drag, then explicitly Confirm cue position. Other potted balls stay down.

Break: eight is respotted, not a win/loss; scratch still gives ball in hand. No four-rail break requirement. 400-shot draw limit. Fixed break position. No spin, timers, jump shots or cushion-shot event requirement.

Rules reviewed against Miniclip support, not an exact Miniclip/Messenger replica. Their special-event rules vary. This game always calls the final pocket. Break-eight respot follows the indexed official multiplayer rules; that older article is no longer live. See README for source limits.
