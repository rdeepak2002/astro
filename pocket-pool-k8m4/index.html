House rules · a quick read

This is shared-table pool, not standard 8-ball. You and Astro alternate one shot each, even after a pot. There are seven numbered object balls; every legal pot is one point, regardless of the number on the ball.

Pot the cue ball and it’s a scratch: no points for that whole shot, and the cue ball is respotted in a free position. Object balls potted on a scratch stay down. Rails and pockets are simplified; there are no called pockets or first-contact fouls.

The round ends when all seven object balls are gone. Higher score wins; equal scores draw. Play Astro here uses a deterministic local computer opponent, not live chat AI. Play Astro in chat waits for you to send the link and open Astro’s reply.
